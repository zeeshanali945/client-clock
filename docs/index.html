Client Clock

A time zone companion for freelancers. See each client's local time, convert deadlines into your own time, and find a time to meet.

Download the Android app (APK) Android only. Free. Works offline.

What it does

  • Live local time for each client, and whether they are in working hours
  • Deadline converter that handles daylight saving changes
  • Reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before a deadline
  • Meeting finder with a message you can copy
  • No location or contacts permission. Your data stays on your phone.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the download button.
  2. Open the downloaded file client-clock.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app, then tap Install.
This app is not from the Play Store, so Android may show a warning. That is normal for apps installed from a file.
